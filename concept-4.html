Wild things, overlooked things, and the quiet abundance of the natural world—preserved as one-of-a-kind botanical art.

Amy Spencer creates art from the materials nature leaves in plain sight. This direction draws from the lushness and warmth of Louisiana without becoming themed or rustic: deep garden green, sun-warmed ivory and aged brass tones create a gracious, collected Southern atmosphere.
