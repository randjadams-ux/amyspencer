About the artist

Amy Spencer

Based in Baton Rouge, Amy uses nature as both medium and muse. Her one-of-a-kind works transform leaves, petals, stems, wildflowers and weeds into compositions that invite a closer look at ordinary beauty.

Her work has been featured in inRegister and is collected through local shops, galleries and private commissions.
